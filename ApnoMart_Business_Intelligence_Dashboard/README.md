# ApnoMart Executive Business Intelligence Dashboard

## Project Overview

This project was developed as part of the **ApnoAI Data Analytics Internship – Task 3: The Insight Board (Business Intelligence Dashboard)**.

The objective is to analyze sales, profit, product categories, regional performance, and customer segments using Power BI and present actionable business insights through an interactive dashboard.

## Tools and Technologies

- Microsoft Power BI Desktop
- Power Query for data preparation
- DAX for KPI calculations
- Data visualization and business intelligence

## Dataset Summary

The dataset contains 1,200 sales records covering May 1 to October 26, 2026.

- **Total Sales:** ₹1,62,61,998.30
- **Total Profit:** ₹44,19,736.17
- **Total Orders:** 1,200
- **Overall Profit Margin:** 27.18%
- **Units Sold:** 6,736

dataset period :May–October 2026

## Dashboard Features

- KPI cards for sales, profit, orders, and profit margin
- Sales analysis by product category
- Sales trends over time
- Profit comparison by region
- Interactive region slicer
- Profitability analysis by category
- Discount versus profit analysis

## Key Business Insights

1. **Revenue Pattern:** Furniture generated ₹35,18,270 in sales, approximately 21.6% of total sales.
2. **Profitability Pattern:** Office Supplies generated the highest category profit at ₹11,76,657.32, while Electronics generated ₹5,58,395.07.
3. **Customer Behavior:** The Corporate segment generated ₹43,11,027.50 in sales, approximately 26.5% of total sales, across 307 orders.

## Strategic Recommendation

Focus on profitable growth by maintaining inventory for strong-selling products, protecting margins in profitable categories, reviewing costs and discounts in lower-profit categories, and monitoring customer-segment performance regularly.
